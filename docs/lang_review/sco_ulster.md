# Ulster Scots (sco_ulster): review notes for the owner (wave 45, session F)

In game: 4 lessons (Linen Hall Library and the Sandwich Bar in Belfast, the Old Mill in Bushmills, the Giant's Causeway
visitor centre). Spellings follow Philip Robinson's "A Test for Ulster-Scots" (Ullans no. 6, 1998, on
ulsterscotslanguage.com), which also gives "ay" for yes and "aye" for always. Mapped zones: Belfast, Bushmills, the
Antrim coast and the Causeway. Derry, Fermanagh, Lough Neagh and the Mournes get no local language by default.

Facts used: settlers from the early 1600s; strongest in Antrim, Down (the Ards), parts of Londonderry and east
Donegal; the 1998 Agreement's line on "the Irish language, Ulster-Scots and the languages of the various ethnic
communities"; the Ulster-Scots Agency (Tha Boord o Ulstèr-Scotch) set up in 1999 under the Agreement; James Orr of
Ballycarry, weaver poet, about 1800.
