# Wave 45 H (Isles of Invention): facts to confirm

Everything below is in the game, written as carefully as I could, but worth a second look by the owner.
Nothing here is invented; these are the lines I'd most like checked against a source.

1. **Chain Home masts at Swingate, Dover** (plaque `dover_chain_home`): "Some of the tall masts of the Swingate
   station still stand". I believe two or three original transmitter masts survive; the plaque avoids a number.
2. **The first ARM chip ran before its power was connected** (plaque `eastanglia_arm`): told as "the story goes",
   from Steve Furber's and Sophie Wilson's own accounts of April 1985.
3. **Ernie Wise's call, 1 January 1985** (plaque `london_first_mobile`): from St Katharine Docks to Vodafone's office
   in Newbury. An earlier private test call the same day is not mentioned, so "first public" is the claim.
4. **AlphaFold's training set** (plaque `eastanglia_alphafold`, Timeline): "about 170,000" Protein Data Bank structures.
   Nobel 2024: half to Demis Hassabis and John Jumper jointly, half to David Baker; the game says they "shared in" it.
5. **Daily Telegraph crossword, 1942** (Bletchley guide): a contest whose fastest solvers were later approached for
   war work.
6. **Colossus** (plaque `bletchley_colossus`): about 1,600 valves in the first (Mark 1), ten by the war's end, at work
   by early 1944, rebuild finished 2007 under Tony Sale.
7. **Geography**: Bletchley Park is reached off Oxford's east road (in reality about 45 km north-east of Oxford, in
   Milton Keynes). Kathleen Lonsdale's plaque is in Dublin and Robert Boyle's and Ernest Walton's are in the Comeraghs
   because Newbridge, Lismore and Dungarvan are not in the game.
8. **Dr Bethan Howells, Dr Rao, Prof. Brennan** are fictional; none is presented as working for DeepMind or any real
   lab.
9. **Jocelyn Bell Burnell's birthplace** (plaque `ln_bell_burnell`): sources differ between Lurgan and Belfast, so the
   plaque says she "grew up in Lurgan" (certain) rather than naming a birthplace. The Timeline says "from Lurgan".
10. **Darwin on the Timeline** (entry `origin_of_species`, found by the existing Shrewsbury statue plaque): On the
    Origin of Species was published on 24 November 1859; the line says "November 1859".
