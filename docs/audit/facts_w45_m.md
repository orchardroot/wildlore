# Wave 45 session M (Borders and neighbours): facts left out or worth a second look

Kept OUT of the game until the owner confirms:
- The 2025 UK/Spain/EU political agreement on the Gibraltar frontier (announced June 2025). The game only says the
  three have had to work out how the frontier runs after Brexit.
- The year the British fence on the isthmus (la Verja) went up (I believe 1908 to 1909).
- La Línea becoming its own municipality in 1870 (separated from San Roque).
- Exact numbers of frontier workers (often quoted as about 15,000 a day); the game says "thousands".
- Northern Cyprus briefly following Turkish time (UTC+3) from September 2016 to late 2017. The game keeps both
  halves of Nicosia on Cyprus time (EET with EU summer time), which has been true since then.
- Real Balompédica Linense's founding year (1912?) and colours.
- Mouflon numbers: the game says "now numbers in the thousands" (estimates I know are about 3,000).

In the game, worth a check:
- "Gibraltar voted 96 per cent Remain, more than anywhere else that voted" (95.9%, the highest of the counting areas).
- Ledra Street crossing opened April 2008; first crossings April 2003; Annan Plan April 2004 (about 65% yes among
  Turkish Cypriots, about 76% no among Greek Cypriots); EU accession May 2004; euro in Cyprus January 2008.
- Halloumi/Hellim registered as an EU PDO in 2021 under both names.
- Selimiye renamed in 1954; Büyük Han built 1572; Kyrenia Gate in the 1560s; Committee on Missing Persons since 1981.
- Pyla described as "one of very few places where Greek Cypriots and Turkish Cypriots still live side by side",
  with two coffee houses (one of each) a few steps apart on the square.
- "The Green Line was drawn in green pencil by a British officer" is given as "the story goes".
- Turkish lira at about 55 to the pound (Forex walk mean 55, 46 to 66). Set from mid-2025 rates; the lira keeps
  falling, so a higher mean may suit 2026.
- 1974 displacement: the plaque says "well over a hundred thousand Greek Cypriots fled south and tens of thousands of
  Turkish Cypriots moved north" (figures usually given are about 160,000 to 200,000 and about 45,000 to 50,000).
- Pilavuna/flaounes: the simit seller says pilavuna is the cheese pastry Greek Cypriots call flaounes and bake at
  Easter. An earlier draft tied the Turkish Cypriot version to Bayram; that was cut as unsure.
- Pyla's Turkish Cypriot coffee-house owner is written as a villager whose family has always been in Pyla (Pyla's
  Turkish Cypriots largely stayed through 1974), not as someone who moved north.
- Committee on Missing Persons: the plaque says "many hundreds" identified (the CMP's own count passed a thousand
  some years ago; "many hundreds" is kept as the safe understatement).
