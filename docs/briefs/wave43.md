## Deep Places (A to E), one each, built with the 3.7 kit (all four puzzle verbs used at least once), multi-floor,
cave-styled, with wild creatures that fit, 4+ accurate plaques, a reward worth the trip, and an entrance from the nearest
existing zone (a cul-de-sac; gate it by story progress if it sits early):
- **A. Wookey Hole** (Somerset, near Glastonbury/Wells): the River Axe, the Witch of Wookey legend, cave-aged cheddar.
- **B. Dan yr Ogof** (the Brecon Beacons, off a South Wales zone): the 1912 discovery by the Morgan brothers.
- **C. The Marble Arch Caves** (Fermanagh, near Enniskillen; extend the existing cave zone if one exists rather than
  duplicating it): the underground river and boat trip.
- **D. Aillwee Cave** (the Burren, near Galway/Doolin): bear bones, the farmer who found it (1944, accurate).
- **E. Fingal's Cave** (Staffa, by boat from Oban or Tobermory): basalt columns, Mendelssohn's Hebrides overture, puffins.

## Casebook mysteries (F to I), one each, 8 to 12 steps, evidence, red herrings, a fair solve, using the case contract,
examine spots and NPCs; funny and fair; historical figures fine, no real living people accused of anything:
- **F. Wales**, **G. Scotland** (not Loch Ness, which exists), **H. Ireland**, **I. Northern Ireland**. Pick a premise
  rooted in a real place and its folklore or history (e.g. a stolen Eisteddfod chair, a vanished Fair Isle jumper
  pattern, a Kilkenny witch-trial echo, a Giant's Causeway stone gone missing).

## J. More festivals (one session): 8+ more real occasions on their real dates using the festivals system, each with an
event, a quest entry and a reward, spread across the nations and territories: e.g. the National Eisteddfod (August,
moving town), Lughnasadh/Puck Fair style harvest fair, Whitby Goth Weekend (spring and autumn), the Notting Hill-style
carnival already exists so skip it, Hogmanay exists, the Braemar Gathering (September), Pancake Day races, Bonfire Night
exists, Tynwald Day on Mann (5 July), Liberation Day in Jersey and Guernsey (9 May), Falklands Liberation Day (14 June),
Bermuda's Cup Match (late July or early August). Accurate dates and customs only.
